### The Business
- **Market:** $12.8B TAM, 17% CAGR, 2.3M self-published titles/year
- **Problem:** Authors spend $5K-17K per book, wait 4-8 weeks
- **Solution:** AI platform delivers professional results in minutes at 10% cost
- **Model:** SaaS subscription ($29-99/month), 87% gross margin
- **Traction:** 50 paying users, 95% AI success rate, 62 NPS

### The Opportunity
- **5-Year Target:** 100K users, $60M ARR, 73% net margin
- **Fundraising:** $2M seed at $10M valuation
- **Break-even:** 18 months (500 users, $25K MRR)
- **Exit:** $50M-300M acquisition or $500M+ IPO

### The Product
- **Suite:** 5 AI-powered tools (Format, Covers, Images, Videos, Voice)
- **Status:** 4 products live, 1 in development (Voice - Q2 2025)
- **Tech:** Next.js, PostgreSQL, OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Runway ML
- **Differentiation:** Integrated, AI-powered, author-first
