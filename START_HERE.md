## 🎓 Key Concepts

### The Three Business Pillars
1. **SaaS Platform** - Monthly subscriptions ($29-99/month)
   - Writer, Format, Covers, Images, Voice tools
   - Predictable recurring revenue
   
2. **Publishing Royalties** - Passive income stream
   - Use our tools to publish our own books
   - Near 100% profit margin
   
3. **Consultative Services** - High-margin execution
   - White-glove service for authors ($1,500-5,000)
   - Use our tools internally (94% profit margin!)

### The Flywheel Effect
```
Free Users → See Our Books → Subscribe to SaaS → Hire for Consulting
     ↑                                                      ↓
     └──────────────── Testimonials & Portfolio ←──────────┘
```

### Zero Trust Architecture
- Every database query filtered by `userId`
- S3 files organized: `users/{userId}/{type}/{file}`
- No cross-user data access possible
- GDPR/CCPA compliant by design
